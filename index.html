What are smart Goals? Smart goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound objectives that help individuals and organizations set clear targets and track progress effectively. They provide a structured framework for goal-setting, ensuring that goals are well-defined and attainable within a certain timeframe. By following the SMART criteria, individuals can increase their chances of success and maintain focus on their desired outcomes.
