What are SMART goals?
SMART goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound objectives that help set clear targets and track progress. They provide a structured framework for goal-setting and can help people stay focused on their desired outcomes. Learn more about the SMART criteria (Boogaard, 2023).
