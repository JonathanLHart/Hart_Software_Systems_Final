About four years ago, I came home to help my father get his life in order. Because of his disabilities, he and his wife were struggling to manage household responsibilities and upkeep. I took a job as an automotive technician at a local dealership so I could help. About two years later, my father passed away.

Later, I injured my back in an accident and had two surgeries. I had to leave my career as a mechanic and could no longer do physical labor. After losing my father and facing the challenges of my injury, I began looking for a new direction. Technology was familiar to me, and I thought I could succeed in IT. I chose software development because I believed it could let me make a meaningful impact while offering flexibility, stability, and good earning potential.

As I thought about my future and retirement, I heard an advertisement that said, “So many people are using AI wrong.” The rest of the message I tuned out, but that line stayed with me. I contacted Western Technical College, where I had studied before, and enrolled in the Software Developer program.

I’m excited about the opportunities ahead and hope to work in AI. Starting over at 57 may seem unusual to some people, but I believe I’m just getting started. I have a lot of life experience and knowledge to share, and I look forward to what comes next.
